## Why it exists

I made this for my sister who provides dog-massage services (yes, its a thing) as an entrepreneur. It is used to keep track of customers and generates reports and receipts for each visit based on the data inputted. It was a nice refresher on Android development, and a more complex project than I had previously done on Android.

## Features

* All data is stored locally on the device, no network access needed
* Each customer can have multiple dogs, who can have multiple visits
* Previous visits can be viewed
* When creating a visit, the report and receipt are generated as PDFs and stored on the device. These can also be sent to the customers email from the app.

## Problems

* Highly custom and non-configurable with hard-coded values, especially the PDF formatting
* I might re-make this as a desktop app for computers
